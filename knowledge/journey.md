# Engineering Journey & Learning Direction

## Engineering Philosophy: "I Build to Understand"

Palleti Vamshi's technical growth is grounded in hands-on building from first principles. Rather than relying on superficial abstractions or black-box libraries without comprehension, Vamshi focuses on understanding how systems operate underneath — whether analyzing lower-bound time complexity in algorithms, modeling physical sensor state machines, or configuring relational schema constraints in relational databases.
